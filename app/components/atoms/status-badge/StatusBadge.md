Borderless pill showing a Tube line's current service status. Local to the tube map, not part of Mahry DS. Figma: Playground → StatusBadge (`77:57`).

White label text on a solid fill. The label itself ("Good Service", "Part Closure") carries the meaning, so status never depends on colour alone.

## Variants

**good** — service running normally (TfL severity 10). Green fill.

**minor** — minor delays (TfL severity 9). Amber fill.

**severe** — anything worse: severe delays, part or full closures, suspensions. Red fill.
